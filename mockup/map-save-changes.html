Open questions

  1. RESOLVED #1 · Cross-city staging

    Staged changes are kept per place, independent of whichever city happens to be on screen — stage a reassignment in Shanghai, switch to Suzhou via the timeline, switch back, and the pin’s gold ring plus its “Unsaved change” pill are exactly as you left them. They also persist across a full reload (this mockup backs them with real localStorage, standing in for the real app’s IndexedDB cache — same durability guarantee as place-detail-modal.html’s drafts, just per staged reassignment instead of per free-text field).

    The Save bar’s count reads as a total across every city, not just the one shown — stage 2 changes in Shanghai, switch to Suzhou (which has none of its own), and the bar stays visible reporting “2 unsaved changes.” (See “#2 · Save vs Discard scope” below for what each of the bar’s two actions actually acts on — they’re not symmetric.)

    Because a Save bar can now be visible with zero unsaved pins in view, a subtle secondary line under the count says where the rest live — “All changes are in Suzhou” when the current city has none of its own, or “+1 more in Suzhou” when it has some too. The timeline’s city nodes also carry a small gold dot when that city has anything pending, so the cue is visible before you even open the bar. This mockup only models 2 of the trip’s 8 legs (Shanghai + Suzhou, reusing their real names/places/days from mockup/mockup.html) — enough to prove the switch-and-persist mechanic; the total/hint logic itself is written generically and holds regardless of how many legs end up with pending edits at once.

  2. RESOLVED #2 · Save vs Discard scope

    Save and Discard deliberately act on different scopes. Save commits every staged change, in every city, in one action — it’s “commit all my work.” Discard only reverts what’s staged in whichever city is currently on screen — it’s a localized “undo what I did here,” not a global undo. Stage changes in both Shanghai and Suzhou, discard from Suzhou, and only Pingjiang reverts: the bar stays up, its count drops by exactly the one change that was cleared, the cross-city hint line and the timeline’s pending-dots update to match what’s left, and Shanghai’s staged change (and its gold-ringed pin) are untouched. The confirm step names the city outright (“Discard 2 unsaved changes in Shanghai?”) so the narrower scope is never ambiguous — the old cross-city discard copy (“…across 2 cities”) no longer applies now that Discard doesn’t reach across cities at all. Because Save and Discard would otherwise look like a plain symmetric pair, the city name also sits on the trigger button itself — “Discard (Shanghai)” — not just inside the confirm dialog, so the asymmetry is visible before the destructive gate, not after.

    Edge case: if the current city has nothing staged but the bar is visible because some other city does, Discard has nothing to act on — rather than leaving it clickable and silently doing nothing, it’s disabled (same real-disabled-attribute treatment as Save mid-flight or offline). The reason is exposed two ways: a title tooltip for mouse users, and a visually-hidden hint span wired up via aria-describedby for keyboard/AT users, who wouldn’t otherwise get a hover to read. The hint line above the button already says where the pending changes actually live, so nobody needs to discover the reason by trial and error.
